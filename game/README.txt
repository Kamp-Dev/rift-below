RIFT BELOW — First dungeon crawler prototype

Open project.godot in Godot 4 and press F5.
WASD / arrow keys: move
Space / J: attack in your movement direction (hold for repeated attacks)
1 / 2 / 3: equip Iron Sword / Rift Cleaver / Frostfang
E / Enter: use the portal after defeating every guardian
R: start a new run after death

Defeated enemies drop gold and grant XP. Levels increase attack strength and
maximum health and restore health. Red potions restore 30 HP automatically
when touched; they remain on the ground if health is full. Each new floor has
nine connected rooms, more powerful enemies, and a portal in the last room.

Uses new south/west/east/north walk and attack views based on the original
Juggernaut. Swords attach separately and can be replaced.
See DIRECTION_AND_WEAPONS.md for equipment setup and weapon stats.

Verified: connected rooms, movement, melee, XP/leveling, descent, death/restart.
